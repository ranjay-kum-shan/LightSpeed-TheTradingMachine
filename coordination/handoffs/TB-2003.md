## Completion Summary

TB-2003 now persists loss-control state, exact signed capital flows, immutable
risk-profile identity, session boundaries, and every transition through the
TB-1006 SQLite boundary. Daily loss excludes deposits and withdrawals. High
water adjusts for documented intraday and opening flows, advances only at a
reconciled close, and still subjects unexplained opening gaps to drawdown.
Daily-loss halts survive restart and improving marks but clear after a closed,
unique new session. Drawdown and generic internal hard halts survive every
public transition, preserve their first reason, and have no reset API.

Every event is revision-contiguous and SHA-256 chained. On each load, the full
chain, current/latest state, and capital-flow ledger/event equivalence are
verified. The effective profile hash must match the initialized scope. Pre-trade
snapshots must explicitly supply flow and durable halt facts; the engine denies
directly from them, while `RiskState.enforce_snapshot` provides the supported
mapping and rejects temporal inversion.

No broker call, alert, real account data, owner reset, or live capability exists.
