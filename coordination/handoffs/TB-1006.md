## Completion Summary

TB-1006 now supplies standard-library-only SQLite and local-file foundations.
Migrations are ordered, exact-text checksummed, committed as one transaction,
recorded in an append-only ledger, and cross-checked against `user_version`.
Public transactions use WAL, full synchronization, foreign keys, a bounded busy
timeout, and `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`; callers receive a restricted facade that cannot
commit early, change durability pragmas, attach another database, expose a
cursor, or mutate migration history. Commit-time failure is distinct and rolls
back. Atomic byte and UTF-8 text writers flush and `fsync` a same-directory
temporary file before `os.replace`, preserving the prior target on failure.

No intent, order, fill, risk, experiment, or reconciliation table is included.
Those schemas remain owned by TB-2003, TB-5002, and later tasks.
