## Objective

Provide one canonical UTC time primitive, a clock port with a deterministic test
clock, and an exchange-session contract, so that every later component depends on
injected time instead of the host wall clock and no naive timestamp can enter the
system.

## Contracts and Requirements

| Contract | Relevant section or invariant | Required behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Requirements](../../docs/01-requirements.md) DATA-003 | Timestamps are timezone-aware, normalized to UTC, and retain exchange-session identity | Naive timestamps are rejected |
| [Requirements](../../docs/01-requirements.md) EXEC-006 | Orders outside the configured session are rejected locally | Exchange calendar boundary tests pass |
| [Market Data Specification](../../docs/04-data-specification.md) | UTC is the internal standard; session dates remain exchange-local; calendars are versioned | Session value carries both UTC bounds and the exchange-local date, and the calendar exposes name and version |
| [Architecture](../../docs/02-architecture.md) | `domain` depends on the standard library only; `adapters` owns clocks; `data` owns calendars | Ports live in `domain`; implementations live in `adapters` and `data` |
| [Testing Strategy](../../docs/09-testing-and-quality.md) | UTC normalization needs naive rejection, offset conversion, DST transitions, and ambiguous local time | All four cases are covered by tests |

Conflicting or missing contract: `None`.

## Scope

### In Scope

- `ensure_utc` as the single naive-rejecting UTC normalization primitive.
- `SessionType` and `ExchangeSession` values with half-open session bounds.
- `Clock` and `ExchangeCalendar` ports declared in `domain`.
- `SystemClock` and deterministic `ManualClock` adapters.
- `StaticExchangeCalendar` as the deterministic calendar implementation.
- Tests for naive rejection, offset conversion, both DST transitions, ambiguous
  local time, session boundaries, holiday, half-day, and calendar validation.

### Out of Scope

- Real holiday and half-day calendar data and pinned calendar versions, which
  TB-3003 owns.
- Wiring session eligibility into `RiskEngine` or the execution path, which
  TB-5004 owns for the full EXEC-006 behavior.
- Consolidating the existing per-module UTC validators in `domain/orders.py`,
  `risk/models.py`, and `operations/controls.py` onto `ensure_utc`.
- Live trading, real credentials, and owner-dependent values.
